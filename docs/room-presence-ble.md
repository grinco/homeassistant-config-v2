# Room presence from the phones (Bluetooth), 2026-11-28

**Goal (operator):** the Home page offers a one-tap shortcut to the room the phone is in, and the
same room reading can later feed Magic Areas presence and other room features.

## How the room is found

The adults' phones are iPhones, and iOS cannot transmit an iBeacon in the background. The six
ESPHome Bluetooth proxies therefore listen for the phone instead: **Private BLE Device** (core)
resolves the iPhone's rotating address from its IRK, taken once from a Mac's Keychain or a Linux
pairing, and **Bermuda** (HACS) turns the proxies' signal strengths into `sensor.<phone>_area`.

Placement matters more than tuning. Plaster walls and floors pass Bluetooth easily, so each proxy
covers a circle, not a room. Live tests on 28.11:
- next to the living-room proxy: Living room, held for minutes;
- in the office: Office, held;
- walking between them: a 6 s Corridor;
- in the open kitchen, which has no proxy: Living room, Closet (through the ceiling), Office and
  Corridor, flipping every few seconds.

The closet proxy carries a −8 dB offset in Bermuda for the floor between it and the ground floor.
The Geiger-counter proxy sits in the office's corner nearest the living room (area fixed 28.11).
A kitchen proxy is the fix for the kitchen; there is no setting that replaces it.

## Settling and the shortcut

`automation.room_shortcut_settle_each_phone_s_room` (`automations/room-shortcut-settle.json`)
copies a phone's area into `input_text.room_<person>` once it has held 30 s, and clears it when the
phone is unknown, unavailable or away. Home's header shows one badge per room page; each badge is
visible only to its user, and only while that person's settled room is that area. Tapping it opens
the room's page. Lint C49 keeps every badge on a real page, limited to one user, and tied to a real
area, with no duplicates.

The settling is a native state trigger with `for:` and cannot be reached by `run.py`; it was checked
live only.

## Presence for Magic Areas

`binary_sensor.phone_presence_living_room` / `_office` (occupancy) are on while anyone's settled room
is that area. Each is assigned to its area and listed in that area's Magic Areas
`keep_only_entities` (operator, 28.11). A keep-only source holds an occupied room occupied, so lights
stay on while someone sits still, but cannot make the room occupied by itself; motion and the FP2
still start presence. Accepted risk: a phone left on the desk keeps an already occupied room occupied
until it moves or leaves.

## Adding a person

Get the phone's IRK into Private BLE Device. Then add a trigger and a `phones` entry to the
automation, create `input_text.room_<name>`, and give each room badge a second copy keyed to the new helper and
user. The badges are generated by a local script that is not committed: it maps the children's
rooms, which this repo never names.
